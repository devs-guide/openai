DOT Research

#RESEARCH is the pure internet-research lane layered on #AGENT. Its files are intentionally compact and grouped by role.

Read left to right

#PROJECT → #INTERNET → #DATA → #TEMPLATE

Optional deliverable: #SUMMARY creates one explicitly requested, evidence-completing subtopic executive brief. It records immutable parent inputs, fills required material Fact gaps through the four-stage workflow, and freezes the final evidence versions before drafting. It is not a fifth required stage.

Document Owns
project.prompt Building, controlling, pausing, resuming, and completing a research project
internet.prompt Completing evidence-based internet research and applying its restrictions
data.prompt Converting reviewed evidence into validated, immutable paired CSV/JSON releases
template.prompt Reusable briefs, records, facts, ledgers, checkpoints, prompts, and outputs
summary.prompt Completing required Facts and producing a self-contained, visually inspected executive-summary PDF for one requested subtopic

Load the #AGENT contract first. These documents may narrow it but cannot grant a model, tool, permission, account, execution surface, or destination.
